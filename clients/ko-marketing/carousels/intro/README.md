# KO Marketing, Instagram intro carousels

Three 6 slide carousels (1080x1350) introducing KO Marketing. Each one is designed as a single
6480x1350 canvas and cut into slides, so torn paper sheets, polaroids, tape, clips and the numbered
note strips (slides 4 and 5) cross the slide borders. The full canvas is exported as `panorama.png`.

| Folder | Post | Cover | Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01-we-are-open](./01-we-are-open/) | We are open | Burgundy | Post first |
| [02-who-we-are](./02-who-we-are/) | Who we are | Cream | Post second |
| [03-what-we-do](./03-what-we-do/) | What we do | Burgundy | Post third |

`previews/` holds one contact sheet per carousel and the feed row (Instagram order, newest on the
left: 03, 02, 01, so the profile row reads burgundy, cream, burgundy).

Every line comes from komarketingagency.com (home, services, plans and contact pages, checked on
3 October 2026). No figures are used on the slides.

## Design system: editorial scrapbook

- Two grounds only: KO burgundy (#4C050C) and cream paper (#F3EBDF). Slides alternate between them
  (carousels 1 and 3 start on burgundy, carousel 2 on cream). Paper grain and a soft vignette come
  from `tex-paper.png` and `tex-soft.png`, and the slide edges are torn paper.
- Scrapbook objects, all kept straight: torn paper notes, polaroid and perforated stamp frames, washi
  tape, paper clips, binder clips, a push pin and a spiral notebook page.
- Collage of real fashion, lifestyle and Bangkok photography plus still life cutouts (rotary phone,
  playing cards, lipstick, red heels, lipstick kiss).
- Type: Playfair Display for titles with one italic accent word per slide, Poppins for kickers and body,
  Caveat for handwritten notes. Big decorative serif letter on the carousel 2 cover, large type set
  on the photo for the carousel 1 cover.
- Official KO logo v3: burgundy lockup on cream slides, white lockup on burgundy slides. The burgundy
  monogram sits in a stamp on carousel 1 slide 3.
- The silk ribbon is not used in this version. Glass pills, glows and UI chrome are gone.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd source
npm i --no-save playwright-core   # uses the system Chrome, override with CHROME=/path/to/chrome
node build.mjs                    # slides, panoramas, captions
python3 sheets.py                 # contact sheets and feed row (needs Pillow)
```
