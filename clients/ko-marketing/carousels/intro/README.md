# KO Marketing, Instagram intro carousels (v3)

Three 6 slide carousels (1080x1350) introducing KO Marketing. Each one is designed as a single
6480x1350 canvas and cut into slides, so the cover photo row, the numbered step cards (slides 4 and 5)
and the tone changes run across the slide borders. The full canvas is exported as `panorama.png`.

| Folder | Post | Cover | Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01-we-are-open](./01-we-are-open/) | We are open | Burgundy | Post first |
| [02-who-we-are](./02-who-we-are/) | Who we are | White | Post second |
| [03-what-we-do](./03-what-we-do/) | What we do | Burgundy | Post third |

`previews/` holds one contact sheet per carousel and the feed row (Instagram order, newest on the
left: 03, 02, 01, so the profile row reads burgundy, white, burgundy).

Every line comes from komarketingagency.com (home, services, plans and contact pages, checked on
3 October 2026). The texts are unchanged from v2. No figures are used on the slides.

## Why v3

v2 copied the look of the reference carousels (scrapbook props, fashion still lifes, handwriting).
v3 starts from KO's own identity instead. From the references it keeps only the principles:
editorial layout, artistic rather than tech, layered depth, real texture, a strong serif hierarchy,
one panorama across the six slides, image led slides and a premium agency feel. None of their props,
motifs, fonts or styling are reused.

## Brand elements taken from KO, and why

| Element | Where it comes from | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Logo v3 (Poppins Bold lockup, burgundy #4C050C) | KO brand kit | Burgundy lockup on white slides, white lockup on burgundy slides, top left on every slide. The white monogram is the hero of carousel 1, slide 3. |
| Burgundy #4C050C | Site buttons, labels and logo; business cards | The dark ground for covers, CTAs and one or two inside slides per carousel. Also the dark pill colour on white slides. |
| White #F5F5F7 and ink #1A1310 | Site background and headline colour | The light ground and the headline colour on light slides. |
| Dusty blue #94B1C8 | Site accent word ("people remember", "journey") and service tags | One accent word per title, the checklist rings, the converging lines. |
| Source Serif 4 Italic | Every headline on the site | All titles, step labels and numerals. |
| Manrope | Site body copy, tags and buttons | Body copy, pills, counter, handle, buttons. |
| ✦ pill labels | Site section labels ("✦ How we work", "✦ Services") | Section label on every slide and the cover chips. |
| Phone frame in front of a row of work | Site hero | Carousel 1 cover, carousel 2 CTA. |
| Rounded photo cards with soft shadow | Site portfolio row, process photo, service cards | The main image format on every slide. |
| Dark numbered circles with italic numerals | Site "How we work" steps | The numbered steps on slides 4 and 5. |
| Frosted cards over the silk | Site stats section | The step cards on carousels 1 and 3, the checklist card, the "Growth" label. |
| Silk ribbon (`silk-element.png`) | Site stats section | Carousel 1 slide 5, carousel 2 slides 1 and 6, carousel 3 slide 5. It is always placed whole: exactly one slide wide with its own edges on the slide edges. It is never cropped, sliced across slides, masked or warped. |
| Burgundy paper grain and tone-on-tone debossed "Knock Out" | KO business cards | The texture of every burgundy slide, with the debossed "Knock Out" set in Source Serif 4 Italic and clipped to its own slide. |
| Service list, plans and process | Site services, plans and home pages | Carousel 3 slide 3 (all 12 services as pills, styled like the site tags), the step cards. |

Grounds alternate so that no two neighbouring covers share a tone, and each carousel opens and closes
on the same tone. Carousels 1 and 3: burgundy, white, burgundy, white, white, burgundy. Carousel 2:
white, burgundy, white, burgundy, burgundy, white.

## Self-check

Each slide was checked against the question "does this look like KO Marketing's brand, or like the
reference template?". All 18 slides are built only from the elements above. There are no polaroids,
stamps, tape, clips, spirals, pins, cutouts, fashion still lifes or handwriting fonts.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd source
npm i --no-save playwright-core   # uses the system Chrome, override with CHROME=/path/to/chrome
node build.mjs                    # slides, panoramas, captions
python3 sheets.py                 # contact sheets and feed row (needs Pillow)
```

Fonts: Source Serif 4 and Manrope, both SIL Open Font License (licences in `source/fonts/`).
