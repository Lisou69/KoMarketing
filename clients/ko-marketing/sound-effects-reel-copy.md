## Research notes

- Short-form editing guides consistently name the same core kit: whoosh or swoosh for transitions, pop or click for text and captions, riser before a reveal, bass drop or impact on the payoff. Record scratch, typing, and camera shutter are used as accents, not on every cut.
- Restraint reads as more professional: sync each effect exactly to the cut and keep it under voice and music. Guides suggest roughly 2 to 4 effects in a 30-second reel.
- Instagram signals for tips content: hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds, saves and DM shares, on-screen text plus captions for sound-off viewing.
- Hashtags are capped at 5 per post and work as topic labels, so use specific tags. The first ~125 characters of the caption show before "more", so lead with the keyword.
- No specific sound packs or brands are named in this copy.
