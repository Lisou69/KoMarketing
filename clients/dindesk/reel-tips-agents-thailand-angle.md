# DinDesk Reel angle: Tips for real estate agents in Thailand

**Format:** English Instagram Reel, 30 to 45 seconds, talking head or face to camera with bold on screen text.
**Pattern:** every tip follows "If you want to X, use Y."
**Offer:** Founding partner. **CTA URL:** dindesk.com/founding-partner

## Strategic angle

Position DinDesk as the agent's daily desk, sitting inside a short list of tools every agent in Thailand already respects. The two Thai portals give the Reel credibility (we are not just selling ourselves), then DinDesk takes over the part of the job those tools do not solve: getting a listing out fast, keeping track of leads, holding every client in one CRM, and never missing a viewing. The viewer leaves with a usable toolkit, and the last three tips land on DinDesk right before the Founding partner CTA.

DinDesk features used (only these): CRM, lead tracking, fast publishing to property portals and social pages, calendar. No numbers, no statistics, no perks of the Founding partner offer beyond its name.

## Hook for the first 2 seconds

1. **On screen:** AGENT IN THAILAND? STEAL THESE. **Spoken:** "Agents in Thailand, steal these tools."
2. **On screen:** STOP LOSING LEADS IN THAILAND **Spoken:** "Agent in Thailand? Stop losing leads."
3. **On screen:** THE THAI AGENT TOOLKIT **Spoken:** "Here is the toolkit every agent in Thailand needs."

**Pick: option 1.** It calls out the exact audience in the first word, "steal" promises instant value with zero effort, and it fits the list format, so viewers stay to collect every tool. Option 2 is sharper on pain but sets up a problem the first two tips (the portals) do not answer. Option 3 is clear but too flat to stop the scroll.

## Recommended order of tips

1. If you want buyers searching in English to find your listing, use DDproperty.
2. If you want to find owner listings and Thai stock to co agent, use Livinginsider.
3. If you want that listing on your portals and social pages fast, use DinDesk.
4. If you want overseas buyers to walk the unit before they fly in, use Matterport.
5. If you want to see where every lead stands, use DinDesk lead tracking.
6. If you want every client in one place, use the DinDesk CRM.
7. If you want zero missed viewings, use the DinDesk calendar.

**Why this order:** it follows the life of a deal (get seen, get stock, publish, show, follow up, organize, close the viewing), opening with names agents already trust so the Reel feels like honest advice, then ending on three DinDesk tips in a row so the product is the last thing in mind when the CTA hits.

Pacing note: hook about 2 seconds, roughly 4 to 5 seconds per tip, CTA about 5 seconds, which lands inside 30 to 45 seconds. Cut tip 2 or tip 4 if the edit runs long.

## Third party tools to cite, and why

1. **DDproperty** (part of PropertyGuru). The best known property portal in Thailand and a strong English browsing experience, so it is the obvious answer for agents chasing expat and foreign buyers. Citing it first makes the Reel feel credible, not salesy.
2. **Livinginsider.** A major Thai portal where a lot of Thai domestic stock and owner listings land, which is exactly where agents look for units to co agent. It shows we know the local market, not just the expat side.
3. **Matterport.** The best known name in 3D virtual tours, with official resellers and capture services in Thailand (Bangkok, Phuket and beyond). It solves a real Thai problem: overseas buyers who want to see a unit before they travel.

Backup option if one of the above is swapped: PropertyHub, a major Thai portal especially strong on rentals.

Do not imply DinDesk publishes to any specific portal named above unless the DinDesk team confirms that integration. Tip 3 says "your portals" on purpose.
