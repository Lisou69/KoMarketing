## 1. Instagram now (Trials / Reels)

### How Trial Reels work (official)
- Toggle **Trial** before Share → shown to **non-followers only**; not on grid / followers’ Feed / Reels tab until you **Share to everyone** (or auto-share). [Instagram for Creators, 10 Dec 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Insights ~**24h**: views, likes, comments, shares (+ Buffer notes saves, replays, watch time / avg watch time). Auto-share option if performing well in first **72h**. [Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018) · [Buffer, 24 Feb 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/)
- Eligibility (Help Center): **public** account; **pro (Business/Creator) ≥200 followers** OR **personal ≥1,000**; must be eligible for recommendations. [Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018)
- Don’t re-upload the same content as a trial — IG may limit reach if it detects previously shared content. [Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018)
- **Compare trials to other trials**, not to normal Reels (cold audience → lower raw views). Mosseri via Buffer: look at sends, likes, watch time across trials. [Buffer, Feb 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/)

### Ranking signals that matter
- **Official Reels predictions** (Mosseri ranking post, May 2023 — still the Meta baseline): likelihood to **reshare**, **watch through**, **like**, and visit **audio page**. Also deprioritize: watermarked / low-res / muted / bordered / **majority-text** / already-posted Reels. [About Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **Mosseri 2025 top-3** (connected + unconnected): **watch time**, **likes per reach**, **sends per reach**. Likes slightly heavier for followers; **sends slightly heavier for non-followers** (discovery). [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **Trials / cold traffic focus for Lisa**: avg watch time, sends/shares, saves, replays, profile visits — **rates**, not raw views. [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/) · [Later, 2 Mar 2026](https://later.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-strategy-how-to-use/) · *3s hold / skip prediction: creator consensus + SocialPilot summaries of IG “watch <3s” prediction — mark as secondary, not Mosseri top-3*
- Saves: useful for tip/drill content (reference value); **not** listed in Mosseri’s 2025 top-3 — track anyway for coaching. *Unverified weight vs sends/watch time.*

### Winning hook formats (patterns, length, text-on-screen)
- Land hook in **~1–3s**; spoken + on-screen text say the **same promise**. [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-strategy-how-to-use/) · creator education consensus (Upgrow / MediaDrop-style guides)
- Patterns that travel: **mistake→fix**, **myth-bust / contrarian**, **specific payoff + timebox** (“give me 30 seconds…”), **identity callout** (“if you play…”) , **list / steal-my-drill**. Specificity > vague curiosity. [Wave Vision A/B guide, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.wavevision.io/blog/how-to-a-b-test-your-instagram-reels) · sports/coach guides (Marketeze, Reelyze)
- Text-on-screen: short (**~5–9 words**), high contrast, **upper-middle** (avoid bottom UI); on frame 1, hold through first ~3s. *Creator consensus / tooling blogs — not Meta official.*
- Sports: show a **glimpse of the payoff/mistake in first 3s** (don’t make them wait for the “money shot”). [Marketeze sports hooks](https://www.marketeze.ai/blog/sports-creators-guide-to-viral-hooks-score-big-with-athletic-content)

### Trials A/B: one-variable rule + what to test
- **One variable only.** Film body once → cut 2–3 openings. Same body, caption, audio, length, CTA — change **only** the first ~1–3s (spoken line and/or overlay). [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-strategy-how-to-use/) · [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/) · [Wave Vision](https://www.wavevision.io/blog/how-to-a-b-test-your-instagram-reels)
- Space variants **~24h+** apart; wait full insights window; promote winner via Share to everyone.
- Highest-leverage first tests for Eric: **hook wording** → then text-on vs voice primacy → then length cut. Don’t trial community/BTS content meant for followers (cold strangers won’t get it). *Strategy consensus (SocialKit/Later)*

### Avoid for coaching / sports education
- **Watermarks / TikTok logos**, low-res, letterbox borders, muted uploads, **majority-text slides**, duplicate/reposted Reels → reduced recommendation eligibility. [About Instagram ranking](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Long warm-ups, logo intros, unexplained full-rally dumps, multi-CTA endings, over-explained lecture opens. [Buffer tips](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/) · sports club Reels guidance (Spized: keep cuts tight)
- Treating Trials as “post everything as trial” with no hypothesis — learn nothing. [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/)

## 2. Padel content that works

### Angles (documented / creator consensus)
- **Relatable beginner chaos > perfect pro points** — people send clips they recognize from their own games. [The Padel Paper / Jack Bailey, 14 Apr 2026](https://thepadelpaper.com/padel-content-creators-jack-riley/)
- **Hook/caption carries the clip** — “why would someone care?” before upload; captions that trigger “that’s exactly my games.” [Padel Paper, Apr 2026](https://thepadelpaper.com/padel-content-creators-jack-riley/)
- Strong coaching angles (global; mix of articles + creator consensus — **not** platform stats):
  - **Technique mistakes** → fix in one beat (return stance, late bandeja, flat vibora, glass-ignorance)
  - **Glass / wall** decisions (let it come / use rebound) — tennis-to-padel myth
  - **POV / on-court** with clear purpose (not silent POV with no hook)
  - **Slow-mo** contact + arrow/text callout
  - **Myth-bust** (“vibora always > bandeja”, “hit harder from back”)
  - **Steal-my-drill** / 30-second patterns partners can try tonight
- Vibora vs bandeja decision content is evergreen in coaching media (when late/off-balance → bandeja; stable short lob → vibora). [Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/the-pitfalls-of-the-vibora-when-the-bandera-makes-all-the-difference/) · [Padelmesh vibora mistakes](https://padelmesh.com/en/articles/common-vibora-mistakes-padel)

### Caption / hook patterns from padel coaching
- Relatable callout: “this is every beginner’s ___”
- Stop-doing: “Stop ___ on the return / at the glass”
- Tennis-crossover myth: “If you play tennis, stop treating padel like ___”
- Decision tree: “Bandeja or vibora? Here’s the 1 check”
- Send-bait: clip friends will forward mid-groupchat (“we do this every Sunday”)
- *Unverified / creator consensus:* face-to-camera + demo cut > pure highlight reel for education accounts

### TH / SEA notes
- Thailand has active clubs/academies (e.g. Bangkok / Pad Thai Padel-style presence) but **no strong public playbook of TH-specific viral hook formulas found** in this research pass.
- **Use global patterns**; localize with Thai/SEA court language, Bangkok club energy, bilingual overlays if Eric’s audience is local — *unverified localized performance.*

## 3. 8–10 concrete hook starters for Eric

*(Coach energy. Fill blanks. Pair spoken + on-screen text. Trial = change only this opening.)*

1. **Mistake callout:** “Stop doing this on your ___ — you’re giving free points.”
2. **Harsh truth:** “I hate to break it to you but your ___ isn’t a power problem — it’s a timing problem.”
3. **Regret / blunt:** “I’m probably going to regret saying this but most players butcher the ___ every single match.”
4. **Myth-bust (adapt ‘scam’ carefully):** “I knew this tip was bad advice the second I saw you ___ at the glass.”
5. **30 seconds:** “Give me 30 seconds to tell you why you need a real ___ (not another tennis swing).”
6. **Steal-my-drill:** “Steal my 30-second ___ drill — partners hate how clean this gets.”
7. **If you like / you’ll love:** “If you like crushing a ___ you’ll LOVE this wall-read before you swing.”
8. **Hot take:** “This might be a hot take but your ___ is killing more points than your serve.”
9. **Hear me out:** “Hear me out — skip the fancy ___ until you can do this off the glass.”
10. **Decision hook:** “Bandeja or vibora? One check in 3 seconds — if you’re late, don’t force the ___.”

**Suggested first Trials pair (one variable = spoken/overlay only):**  
A: #2 harsh truth on *vibora* · B: #6 steal-my-drill on same technique body.
