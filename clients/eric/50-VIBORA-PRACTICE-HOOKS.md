# 50 written hooks — Eric vibora practice (Instagram Trials / Reels)

Date: 2026-09-24  
Use: on-screen text = spoken line (same promise). Target **5–9 words**. Upper-middle. Mistake or payoff visible in first 3s.

## What research favors (written phrases)

- **Short overlay** (~5–8 words) equals the spoken promise — mute-first feeds. (Creator consensus / Blitzcut 2026, Reelyze fitness hooks)
- **Mechanism > vague promise** (“late feet = late brush” beats “fix your vibora”). (CreatorHouse fitness 2026)
- **Mistake / myth / number / steal-my / send-to** patterns still travel for coaches. (Coachway, SuperDirector coaching hooks)
- **Share frame** (“send this to…”) aligns with Reels discovery weighting sends. (Blitzcut 2026; Mosseri 2025 sends/reach)
- Vibora content that lands: power≠control, sidespin not slap, space, early prep, late contact. (Padelmesh vibora mistakes; Martín Canali / The4Set notes)

Avoid as openers: “Hey guys”, logo intros, “stop scrolling if…”, unsupported “secret pro tip”.
