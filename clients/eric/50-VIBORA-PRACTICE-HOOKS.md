## 4) The 50 phrases (ready to put on screen)

### A. Mistake callouts (1–10)
1. Stop killing your vibora with late prep.
2. You’re standing too close — give the ball space.
3. That flat swing isn’t a vibora.
4. Stop snapping the wrist first.
5. You’re solving the shot too late.
6. Don’t force a vibora off a bad lob.
7. Your elbow drops — keep it high.
8. Stop aiming for power at 100%.
9. You’re missing the side of the ball.
10. Stop watching after contact — close the net.

### B. Myth-bust / harsh truth (11–18)
11. I hate to break it to you — vibora ≠ smash harder.
12. Your vibora isn’t weak. Your setup is.
13. This tip is bad advice: “always go vibora.”
14. I knew that tip was wrong when you went for glory at the glass.
15. This might be a hot take — bandeja wins more points.
16. Hear me out — skip fancy spin until you’re balanced.
17. I’m probably going to regret saying this — most viboras should be safer.
18. I don’t mean to sound harsh but that wrist is costing you.

### C. Timing / decision (19–26)
19. Bandeja or vibora? One check in 3 seconds.
20. If you’re late, don’t force the vibora.
21. Short stable lob → vibora. Late / off-balance → bandeja.
22. Give me 30 seconds on when NOT to vibora.
23. Read earlier. Prepare earlier. Commit.
24. Under pressure? Middle vibora, not corners.
25. Timing problem, not power problem.
26. Feet first. Spin second.

### D. Steal-my / drill / practice (27–34)
27. Steal my 30-second vibora drill.
28. Steal my wrist-only vibora warm-up.
29. Steal my cone-target vibora practice.
30. Practice the brush at 3 o’clock — slow.
31. Film your vibora. You’ll see the flat contact.
32. 5 progressive vibora drills — start here.
33. Partners hate how clean this drill gets.
34. Same feed. Fix one variable tonight.

### E. Identity / tennis crossover (35–40)
35. If you play tennis, stop swinging through it.
36. Tennis smash energy is killing your vibora.
37. Intermediate padel players: this is your vibora leak.
38. For club players stuck at the same level — watch this.
39. If you keep netting the vibora, read this.
40. Bangkok club night energy: fix this before Sunday.

### F. Curiosity / pattern interrupt (41–45)
41. You’re not ready for this vibora fix.
42. So this just happened in practice…
43. You are not going to believe this contact point.
44. Repeat after me: space, brush, finish.
45. Before you cancel me — bandeja first.

### G. Promise / payoff (46–50)
46. Give me 30 seconds to fix your vibora.
47. One change. Cleaner sidespin. More pressure.
48. If you like a heavy vibora you’ll LOVE this wall-read.
49. Before / after: same lob, different contact.
50. This will either annoy you or free your vibora.
