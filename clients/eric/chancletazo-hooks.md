# Eric – Chancletazo hooks (IG Trials)

**The shot:** The chancletazo (Spanish for "a slap with a flip-flop") is an aggressive, flat forehand hit close to the net on a high, floating ball between net and shoulder height, struck with a short, fast swing and contact in front of the body to finish the point.

## Part 1 – Regular hooks

1. Spanish for flip-flop slap. Padel for point over.
2. Float it to Eric at the net. Mistake.
3. High ball, short swing, no reply.
4. Flat face, fast hands, zero reaction time.
5. This is what a chancletazo looks like.
6. Leave it shoulder high and this happens.
7. No slice, no spin, just chancletazo.
8. Contact in front, weight forward, ball gone.
9. Aim at the body, not the lines.
10. Coello hits these. So does Eric.
