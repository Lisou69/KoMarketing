## 1) Gen Z / Gen Alpha–native patterns winning on Reels (2025–2026)

| Pattern | Template shape | Why it works | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------------|--------|
| **POV-as-advice** (not lecture) | `POV: you figured out how to [desirable outcome]` · `POV: [aspirational state]` | Advice slips past “being taught”; feels like a scenario | Captain Hook AI — *4 Reels Hook Formats 2026*; Reddit analysis of 1000+ hooks (2025) |
| **Hyper-specific scene** | `The way I [tiny specific behavior] when [moment]` | Specificity > guru phrases; “story mode” not ad mode | CreatorHouse — *6 patterns 2026*; Blitzcut Reels hooks 2026 |
| **Soft flex / aspirational POV** | `POV: your [sport moment] looks like this` · main-character energy | Share + aspiration without flex-bro tone | Blitzcut 2026 (aspirational POVs among most-shared) |
| **“Feels illegal to know” energy** | `Feels illegal how clean this [shot] looks` | FOMO + curiosity without niche gatekeeping | Blitzcut 2026 (named high-performing phrase family 2025–26) |
| **Self-aware / weaponized honesty** | `Be so for real…` · `Not me romanticizing ___` | Anti-guru; sounds like a friend | Captain Hook AI 2026 (weaponized self-awareness format) |
| **Scenario / skit cold open** | Set a scene people already imagined | Sports creators winning with scenes not tips | @benlikessports strategy write-up (Content Labs) — scenario > hot take lecture |
| **Partner / third-person spark** | `Wait tell them what you just said about ___` | Curiosity without coach voice | Common Reels format (2025–26 creator education) |

**Length / craft (phrase rules):**
- Spoken + on-screen same line; mute-first (RE/UGC 2026).
- Often **8–15 words** OK on IG (Blitzcut / Teleprompter ~7–12; Captain Hook notes IG avg hooks ~15 words vs TikTok longer).
- Land in **first 1–3s**; authenticity > polished guru (Blitzcut citing Meta 2025 authenticity bias).
- **Avoid as AI-tells in 2026:** “Are you tired of…”, stacked “unpopular opinion”, millennial “Stop doing this” lecture openers (CreatorHouse 2026).

## 3) 22 ready phrases for a vibora practice clip  
*(Desire / POV / humor / soft flex. No shame. No save/send. No clinic voice. Vibora can stay as one cool word on screen — or swap to “that snake shot” / “this overhead” if Lisa wants zero jargon.)*

### POV / desire
1. POV: you finally get that snake spin and the court goes quiet.
2. POV: your vibora looks this clean on a random Tuesday practice.
3. POV: you figured out the soft power version and now it’s unfair.
4. POV: main character energy but make it padel.
5. POV: the lob comes up and you already know it’s yours.

### Soft flex / aesthetic
6. Feels illegal how clean this vibora looks in slow mo.
7. The vibora era I romanticized and then accidentally unlocked.
8. Not me treating practice like a music video.
9. Soft power. Heavy spin. Zero rush.
10. This is what “just practicing” looks like when it hits.

### Humor / self-aware
11. Be so for real… I could watch this contact all day.
12. The way I get unserious the second the vibora connects.
13. Me pretending this was casual when I’ve been grinding it all week.
14. Not the vibora making me feel famous for 0.5 seconds.
15. The delusion of thinking I’m chill about this shot.

### Scene / identity
16. The way the ball kisses the glass after this one.
17. Club night energy but it’s just me and a basket of balls.
18. Bangkok heat, cold vibora, no notes.
19. When the practice feed is mid and you still paint the corner.
20. That little smile after a heavy one — yeah that.

### Tease / spark (no lecture)
21. Wait… play that contact again.
22. If you know you know — this shot hits different.
