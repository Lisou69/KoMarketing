Double glass. Don't panic.

Elisabeth on glass defense — the back wall, and the corner bounce (double glass) that kills the rally.

1. Read the glass first. Back wall stays on line. Double glass (two walls, the corner) comes out late and changes direction.
2. Don't chase it. The glass gives you time. Step in too early and the rebound eats you.
3. Reset, then play. First ball off the glass stays in the rally. The winner comes after.
