Elisabeth on the chancletazo — the flat forehand finish when you’re glued to the net and the ball sits up.

1. Read the green light. Ball between net and shoulder height, in front of you, weight going forward. Anything lower or late is a reset, not this shot.
2. Hit flat to finish. This is not a soft volley and not a bandeja. Stable face, contact in front, drive through — body, hip, or the gap.
3. Wrong ball stays soft. Reaching, falling back, or a dropping ball = net or glass. Selection beats power.
