# Notes — Elisabeth, chancletazo

## Rationale

- **Topic:** chancletazo (ES: “flip-flop slap”) — aggressive flat forehand finish very close to the net on an easy, floating ball around net-to-shoulder height. Not a bandeja, not a vibora, not a soft holding volley.
- **Why this hook:** players soft-volley balls they should end. Lisa rejected ultra-short 4–7 word cards (2026-09-24); opener is a full spoken/POV line: **POV: that floaty ball at the net and you finally finish it flat.**
- **Keys are reads, not vibes:** (1) green-light selection, (2) flat finish through the ball, (3) wrong ball stays soft. Matches a technique clip that shows when to go — and when not to.
- **Voice:** Direct — direct, no fluff, no “In this video”. Longer spoken/POV openers (not punch cards). Elisabeth is the demonstrator; brand is the credit, not the hero of the first line. No command save/send CTAs.
- **FR:** rewrite, not a calque. Keep `chancletazo` as the shot name (standard ES term). Avoid literal “tong” / “claquettes” on screen.

## Not this shot

| Shot | Role |
|------|------|
| Soft / holding forehand volley | Keep the net, stay low |
| Bandeja | Controlled overhead after a deep lob |
| Vibora | Aggressive spun overhead on a shorter lob |
| Chancletazo | Close-net flat finish on an easy shoulder-height ball |
